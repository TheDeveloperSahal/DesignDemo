FAQs

Quick answers about reading, publishing, and keeping up with PALLETDOCS.

What is PALLETDOCS?

An independent publication about technology, design, engineering, and the craft of building thoughtful digital products.

How often do you publish?

New articles go out every week. The homepage collects them into fresh reads, trending stories, guides, and long-form essays.

Are the articles free to read?

Yes. Every article on the site is free. The newsletter simply brings the best new reads straight to your inbox.

Can I contribute an article?

We welcome thoughtful pitches from writers and practitioners. Send a short outline through the newsletter form and tell us what you would like to explore.

How do I stop getting emails?

Every email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click is all it takes, no questions asked.
